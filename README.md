#PHP QUADRATIC SOLVER AND OTHER LOGIC EXPRESSIONS
In this project, I am solving quadratic equations and other Mathematics related problems.
In the project, I will use the quadratic formula to solve the quadratic equation.
Built with PHP.
The quadratic formula is a closed-form expression describing the solutions of a quadratic equation.
The same solutions can be achieved with completing square method.
With x representing an unknown, and the coefficients a,b and c representing known real or complex numbers with a being equal to zero or not.
The values of x satisfying the equation, called the roots or zeros can be found using the quadratic formula where the plus-minus symbol indicates that the equation has two roots.
The quantity inside the square root is known as the discriminant.
Geometrically,the roots represent the x values at which the graph of the quadratic function, a 'parabola' crosses the x-axis.
